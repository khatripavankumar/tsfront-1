How AssessAI Works

Four AI-powered steps from job role to hiring decision

1
Define the Assessment

Enter a job title, select required technologies, choose experience level and question count. AssessAI constructs a structured prompt visible to you.

🤖 AI Prompt
2
AI Generates Questions

The model returns MCQs with 4 options, coding challenges with context, a system design question, and a behavioral question — all with expected answers and difficulty ratings.

🤖 AI Output
3
Candidate Takes It

Candidate receives a timed link. They work through questions in a clean portal with a code editor, live timer, progress navigator, and auto-save every keystroke.

4
AI Evaluates & Reports

Every answer is scored 0–10 with evidence-based reasoning. A final report includes hire recommendation, confidence score, skill gaps, and suggested interview questions.

🤖 AI Scoring
🔒Evidence-based scoring only
🎯No demographic inference
👤Human override on every score
📋Full audit trail
🖨Print-ready reports
