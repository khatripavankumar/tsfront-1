How AssessAI Works
Four steps, each powered by AI
1
You define the role and requirements 🤖 AI Prompt
HR enters the job title, required tech stack, experience level, and number of questions. AssessAI constructs a structured prompt and sends it to the AI model to generate a tailored assessment. The prompt is visible so you can understand what the AI is doing.
2
AI generates a full assessment in seconds 🤖 AI Output
The model returns structured questions: multiple-choice conceptual questions with 4 options, coding challenges with problem statements and test cases, a system design question, and a behavioral question. Each question includes difficulty rating and expected time.
3
Candidate takes the assessment in a clean portal
The candidate receives a timed link. The portal provides a live countdown timer, a question navigator for easy jumping between questions, appropriate input types (code editor for coding questions, radio for MCQ), and auto-save on every change.
4
AI evaluates every answer with reasoning 🤖 AI Scoring
After submission, the AI evaluates each answer independently: assigns a 0–10 score, explains exactly why with evidence from the candidate's answer, flags knowledge gaps, suggests interview follow-ups, and generates a final hire recommendation with confidence score.
